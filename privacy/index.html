Website Privacy Notice

Updated November 2, 2026

This notice covers visits to the Resolve Quantum website and initial inquiries made through its contact options. For information about the handling of documents in an appraisal or consulting engagement, contact our team before sending those documents.

Information you choose to provide

The contact form asks for your name, email address, an optional phone number and a message. Information you provide in an inquiry is used to respond to your request and discuss the services you need. Please provide only the information needed for that conversation; avoid submitting sensitive claim records or information about other people in an initial inquiry.

Website hosting and embedded services

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The contact form is hosted by Google Forms. Demonstrations on the site include Vimeo, DJI and DocuSketch, and the site loads a font from Google Fonts. When your browser loads these services, their providers receive connection information such as your IP address and browser details and may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. Loading may occur as you scroll to an embedded demonstration or form.

Information processed by these providers may be handled outside Canada. See GitHub's privacy statement, Google's privacy policy, Vimeo's privacy policy, DJI's privacy policy and DocuSketch's privacy policy for their practices.

Analytics

No Google Analytics or advertising tracking tag is configured on this website. Embedded third-party services have their own data practices, as described above.

Privacy questions and requests

For questions about the use or retention of information you have provided, or to request access, correction, deletion or withdrawal of consent, email Admin@resolvequantum.ca or call 250-895-9381. Describe your request without including sensitive documents. Any response will depend on the information involved and applicable obligations, including Canadian privacy law such as PIPEDA where it applies.

You can also find information about privacy rights and complaints through the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
